# 隐私政策（Privacy Policy）

**生效日期：2026年4月20日**

“本应用”高度重视您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何处理您的信息。

## 1. 数据收集与使用

### 1.1 个人信息
我们默认不收集可直接识别个人身份的信息（如姓名、手机号、电子邮箱、账号密码、激活码等）。

### 1.2 本地数据
订阅源、播放记录、设置偏好等数据主要保存在您的设备本地，用于应用功能实现。

### 1.3 使用数据统计（可选）
仅在您首次启动并明确同意后，本应用才会收集基础匿名使用事件（如页面访问、搜索、播放操作、应用前后台状态）用于改进功能与稳定性。  
您可在 **“设置 > 法律与隐私 > 使用数据统计”** 随时关闭；关闭后将停止上报并清除本地待上传统计队列。

### 1.4 第三方内容
本应用支持用户自行导入第三方订阅源。您访问第三方内容时，相关数据处理受对应第三方隐私政策约束，本应用无法控制且不承担责任。

## 2. StoreKit（应用内购买）

本应用使用 Apple 提供的 StoreKit 处理订阅与支付。支付信息、订单信息等由 Apple 处理；本应用仅接收必要的订阅状态结果用于会员功能解锁。

## 3. 设备权限

- **网络权限**：用于加载您订阅的内容、请求必要的服务接口。  
- **本地存储权限**：用于保存播放进度、订阅配置与应用设置。

## 4. 数据安全与保留

我们采取合理措施保护数据安全。匿名使用统计数据仅用于产品改进，并按业务需要保留在合理期限内。

## 5. 政策变更

我们可能根据产品迭代或法律要求更新本政策。更新后将通过应用内或相关页面公布，建议您定期查看。

## 6. 联系我们

如您对本政策有疑问，可通过应用内提供的联系方式与我们联系或发送邮箱到：wanyneduan30@gmail.com

# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: April 20, 2026**

(“the App”) values your privacy. This Policy explains how we handle your information.

## 1. Data Collection and Use

### 1.1 Personal Information
By default, we do not collect directly identifiable personal information (such as name, phone number, email address, account password, or activation code).

### 1.2 Local Data
Data such as subscriptions, watch history, and preferences is primarily stored locally on your device for core app functionality.

### 1.3 Usage Analytics (Optional)
Only after you explicitly consent on first launch, the App collects basic anonymous usage events (such as screen views, searches, playback actions, and app foreground/background state) to improve features and stability.  
You can disable this anytime in **Settings > Legal & Privacy > Usage Analytics**. Once disabled, uploads stop and the local pending analytics queue is cleared.

### 1.4 Third-Party Content
The App allows users to import third-party subscription sources. When you access third-party content, data processing is governed by those third parties’ privacy policies, which are outside our control and responsibility.

## 2. StoreKit (In-App Purchases)

The App uses Apple StoreKit for subscriptions and payments. Payment and order details are processed by Apple. The App only receives the minimum subscription status result required to unlock premium features.

## 3. Device Permissions

- **Network Access**: used to load subscribed content and required service endpoints.  
- **Local Storage**: used to save playback progress, subscription configuration, and app settings.

## 4. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect data security. Anonymous analytics data is used only for product improvement and retained for a reasonable period as needed.

## 5. Policy Changes

We may update this Policy due to product updates or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published in-app or on related pages. Please review periodically.
