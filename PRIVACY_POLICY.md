# 映盒隐私政策

**更新日期：2026 年 10 月 10 日**

本政策说明映盒（YingBox）iOS 应用如何处理您的信息，以及您可以如何管理相关设置、授权和数据。

## 1. 本地数据与 iCloud

媒体库配置、订阅配置、播放进度、收藏和偏好主要保存在您的设备上，用于实现媒体浏览、播放和个性化功能。

如果您开启 iCloud 同步，应用支持同步的数据也会存储在您的 Apple iCloud 账号中。相关云服务由 Apple 提供。

您可以通过应用设置清理支持删除的本地记录和缓存，并管理 iCloud 同步设置。

## 2. 会员、设备与通知服务

为了管理设备和提供必要的服务，映盒服务器会接收设备标识、机型、系统与 App 版本、语言及时区等信息。

应用内购买和订阅支付由 Apple StoreKit 处理。映盒会使用必要的 Apple 交易信息和会员状态信息进行会员验证与功能解锁。

如果您使用邮箱恢复会员功能，服务器会接收您提交的邮箱、验证码和设备标识，用于验证与恢复已有会员权益。

如果您开启通知，服务器会接收推送令牌及通知设置，用于向您的设备投递通知。您可以在系统设置中管理通知权限。

## 3. AI 数据处理与您的同意

AI 找片和 AI 精选是可选功能。使用这些功能前，应用会说明共享的数据范围，展示当前配置的数据接收服务，并征求您的明确同意。

获得同意后，映盒服务器会处理本次请求，并将生成结果所需的内容转交给说明页列出的 AI 服务。发送内容包括：

- 您输入的本次需求文字；
- 候选影片的片名、简介、题材和用于匹配结果的临时候选编号；
- 您开启题材口味开关时，生成结果所需的题材偏好摘要。

应用不会主动将订阅源地址、订阅源密码或令牌、原始观看记录或视频文件附带给 AI 服务。请勿在需求文字中输入个人信息、密码或其他敏感内容。

请求可能由说明页列表中的可用服务处理，包括故障时的备用服务。AI 服务会按照其适用的政策处理收到的内容。接收服务变化后，应用会重新征求您的同意。无法核实接收服务或未获得当前有效同意时，应用不会发送本次找片需求和候选数据。

您可以拒绝使用 AI，继续使用普通搜索和推荐。也可以在 **设置 → 隐私与安全 → AI 数据共享** 中撤回同意。撤回后，应用停止发起新的 AI 请求；撤回无法收回已经发送的数据，也不会自动删除 AI 服务已经处理的数据。

## 4. 可选使用统计

使用数据统计默认关闭。仅在您明确同意后，应用才会发送设备和会话标识、设备与 App 信息，以及页面访问、搜索操作、播放操作、应用前后台状态等交互事件，用于改进功能和稳定性。

这些统计不用于广告追踪。由于统计包含设备和会话标识，我们不会将其表述为完全匿名的数据。

您可以在应用设置中关闭 **使用数据统计**。关闭后，应用停止上报并清除本地待上传统计队列。

## 5. 第三方连接与内容

映盒本身不提供视频内容，也不出售第三方片库访问权限。

当您连接自己的媒体服务器、网盘、Trakt 账号或其他自行配置的来源时，应用会向您选择的服务发送连接和实现对应功能所需的信息。相关服务对数据的处理也适用其各自的隐私政策。

请在连接第三方服务或导入订阅源前了解其数据处理方式。

## 6. 数据保留、查阅与删除

本地记录和缓存可通过应用中提供的设置清理。服务器数据会根据会员验证、安全和服务运行所需保留，并可能受到适用的法定保留义务约束。

您可以通过下方联系方式请求查阅或删除映盒服务器保存的相关数据。处理请求时，我们可能需要核实您的身份或数据归属，避免将他人的数据提供给您或误删。

映盒服务器的数据请求不等同于删除您的 Apple iCloud 数据、Apple 交易记录或第三方服务已经接收的数据。相关数据的管理方式也取决于对应服务。

## 7. 政策更新与联系我们

我们可能根据功能变化或数据处理方式的调整更新本政策，并通过应用内或公开政策页面公布更新后的内容。

隐私及数据请求邮箱：**support@yingbox.app**。

# YingBox Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 10, 2026**

This Policy explains how the YingBox iOS app handles your information and how you can manage related settings, permissions, and data.

## 1. Local Data and iCloud

Library settings, source configurations, playback progress, favorites, and preferences are primarily stored on your device to support browsing, playback, and personalization.

If you enable iCloud synchronization, data supported by this feature will also be stored in your Apple iCloud account. The relevant cloud services are provided by Apple.

You can use the app's settings to clear supported local records and caches and manage iCloud synchronization.

## 2. Membership, Device, and Notification Services

To manage devices and provide necessary services, YingBox servers receive information such as a device identifier, device model, operating system and app versions, language, and time zone.

In-app purchases and subscription payments are handled through Apple StoreKit. YingBox uses necessary Apple transaction information and membership status information to verify membership and enable features.

If you use email-based membership recovery, the server receives the email address, verification code, and device identifier you submit to verify and recover existing membership access.

If you enable notifications, the server receives a push notification token and notification settings to deliver notifications to your device. You can manage notification permissions in system settings.

## 3. AI Data Processing and Your Consent

AI Search and AI Picks are optional features. Before using them, the app explains the data to be shared, displays the currently configured recipients, and requests your explicit consent.

After you consent, YingBox servers process your request and forward the information needed to generate results to the AI services listed in the disclosure. This information includes:

- The text of your current request;
- Candidate titles, descriptions, genres, and temporary candidate identifiers used to match results;
- A summary of genre preferences needed for the result, only if you enable the corresponding preference option.

The app does not automatically attach source addresses, source passwords or tokens, raw viewing history, or video files to the information sent to AI services. Do not enter personal information, passwords, or other sensitive information in your request.

A request may be processed by an available service listed in the disclosure, including a backup service if another service fails. AI services process the information they receive under their applicable policies. The app requests consent again when the recipients change. If recipients cannot be verified or valid consent for the current disclosure has not been obtained, the app does not send your current request or candidate data.

You can decline AI features and continue using ordinary search and recommendations. You can also withdraw consent under **Settings → Privacy and Security → AI Data Sharing**. Withdrawal stops new AI requests from the app. It cannot retrieve data already sent and does not automatically delete data already processed by an AI service.

## 4. Optional Usage Analytics

Usage analytics are disabled by default. Only after you explicitly consent does the app send device and session identifiers, device and app information, and interaction events such as page visits, search actions, playback actions, and foreground or background transitions. These data are used to improve features and stability.

These analytics are not used for advertising tracking. Because they include device and session identifiers, we do not describe them as fully anonymous.

You can disable **Usage Analytics** in the app's settings. Doing so stops uploads and clears the local queue of analytics events awaiting upload.

## 5. Third-Party Connections and Content

YingBox does not itself provide video content or sell access to third-party media catalogs.

When you connect your own media server, cloud storage, Trakt account, or another source you configure, the app sends the information necessary to connect to that service and perform the relevant functions. Those services also handle data under their own privacy policies.

Review a third-party service's data practices before connecting it or importing its source configuration.

## 6. Retention, Access, and Deletion

Local records and caches can be cleared using the settings provided in the app. Server data are retained as needed for membership verification, security, and service operation and may be subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

You can contact us below to request access to or deletion of relevant data stored on YingBox servers. We may need to verify your identity or ownership of the data to avoid disclosing or deleting another person's information.

A request concerning YingBox server data does not itself delete your Apple iCloud data, Apple transaction records, or data already received by third-party services. Management of those data also depends on the relevant service.

## 7. Policy Updates and Contact

We may update this Policy when features or data practices change. The updated version will be made available in the app or on the public policy page.

For privacy questions and data requests, contact **support@yingbox.app**.
